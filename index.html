Enrollment by academic level, post-study work (OPT), institution type, gender and economic impact. Every number comes straight from an official publication, and each card links to its source. Nothing here is estimated.

Read this first: what the numbers count
  • Open Doors (IIE / U.S. State Dept.) counts students by academic year (e.g. 2024/25) and includes students on OPT.
  • SEVIS by the Numbers (DHS / ICE) counts every active F-1/M-1 record in a calendar year, so it runs higher than Open Doors. The two are shown separately and never added together.
  • Master's degrees: neither source publishes a master's-only count for Indian students. Open Doors' "Graduate" category (master's + doctoral + professional) is the closest official figure, so that is what this dashboard shows. Nationally, master's students are the largest higher-ed group in SEVIS (46.5% of all foreign degree seekers in 2024).
