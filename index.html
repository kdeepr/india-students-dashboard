Data Sources & Methodology
All data sourced from official government and institutional publications. Master's estimates derived at ~85% of graduate enrollment (per NSF NCSES and SEVIS graduate-level breakdowns).

• IIE Open Doors 2024 / 2025: opendoorsdata.org — Primary source for annual enrollment by country and level of study.
• SEVIS by the Numbers 2024 (ICE/DHS): ice.gov/sevis — Active F-1 visa records, state and field breakdowns.
• DHS Study in the States: studyinthestates.dhs.gov — SEVIS quarterly reports and trend analysis.
• NSF NCSES: ncses.nsf.gov — Gender breakdown, STEM field distribution for international graduate students.
• NAFSA: nafsa.org — Economic value and enrollment trend data.
• Dashboard built June 2026 · Data current through 2024–25 academic year.
